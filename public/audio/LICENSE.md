# Audio credits

`birds.webm`, `footsteps.webm` and `bleats.webm` are **CC0 (public domain)** recordings by
Joseph Sardin, [BigSoundBank](https://bigsoundbank.com). They were cut and
encoded by `npm run audio` (`scripts/build-audio.mjs`) from sources kept in
`assets-src/audio/` (not shipped).

| File | Source |
|---|---|
| `birds.webm` | [Evening Birds](https://bigsoundbank.com/evening-birds-s1859.html) (#1859): a 90 s seamless loop |
| `footsteps.webm`, `footsteps.json` | [Steps in the Grass, Slow](https://bigsoundbank.com/detail-1253-steps-in-the-grass-slow.html) (#1253) and [Steps in the Grass, Quick](https://bigsoundbank.com/steps-in-the-grass-quick-s1254.html) (#1254): single steps, walking and running |
| `bleats.webm`, `bleats.json` | [Bleating Goat #1](https://bigsoundbank.com/bleating-goat-1-s0279.html) (#0279), [Bleating goat #2](https://bigsoundbank.com/bleating-goat-2-s0280.html) (#0280) and [Dwarf goat bleating](https://bigsoundbank.com/dwarf-goat-bleating-s0880.html) (#0880): nine single bleats, the goat answering a call |

`wind.webm` is the project's own wind recording (source in `assets-src/music/`).
